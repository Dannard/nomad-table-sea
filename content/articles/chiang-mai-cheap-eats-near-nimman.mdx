## Nimman is convenient, but not always cheap

Nimman has everything in one area, but prices can creep up fast when you eat only on the main strips. A better strategy is to anchor yourself in Nimman for work and step out 10 to 15 minutes for food.

## Budget rule that keeps quality high

If a place serves both students and office workers during weekday lunch, quality is usually consistent. Prioritize places with fast table turnover and limited menus.

## Best cheap lunch pattern

Pick one dish-heavy spot, not a cafe menu. Northern Thai food wins on value and flavor depth, especially soups and braised pork rice.

## Dinner options that still feel local

Markets near the old city edge are better for variety than mall food courts. You can rotate proteins, add fresh greens, and keep costs predictable across the week.
