## Avoiding the obvious queue spots

Canggu can feel noisy if you only follow popular reels. Better cafes are often one or two turns away from the main flow, with better seating and less waiting.

## A simple selection filter

Check table depth, power access, and fan placement before you order. Most problems for remote workers are physical setup issues, not menu quality.

## Food quality vs. work quality

Some cafes are great for food but weak for concentration. Treat each place as either a focus location or a social meeting point and plan accordingly.
