## District 1 after 9 PM

Even after coworking spaces close, District 1 still gives you strong options if you know which streets have real local turnover. The key is avoiding places designed mainly for one-time tourist traffic.

## How to choose in five minutes

Look for short menus and visible prep stations. If locals are ordering in waves, quality is usually more consistent than polished all-day restaurants.

## A reliable late-night sequence

Start with noodles for something hot and filling, then move to a lighter banh mi if you still need fuel for a final work block.

## Safety and comfort tips

Carry small cash, stay on lit streets, and pick seating with clear visibility if you're dining solo with a laptop bag.
