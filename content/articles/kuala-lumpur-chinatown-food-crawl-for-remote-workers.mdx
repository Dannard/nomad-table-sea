## Why Chinatown works after work hours

You get density, variety, and strong value in a walkable zone. That combination is ideal when your workday ends late and you still want an intentional meal plan.

## Recommended crawl pattern

Start with one signature bowl, then move to a shareable second stop. This gives you both local depth and flexibility if you are eating with other nomads.

## Keep logistics simple

Bring cash for hawker lanes, use transit for entry/exit, and avoid carrying full laptop gear if you're planning crowded food streets.
